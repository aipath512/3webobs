Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026 · Operator: AIVENTURE S.R.L., Romania

This page explains how 3webobs.com uses cookies and similar local storage technologies.

Short version. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not track you across other websites. The site uses a small number of strictly necessary items, plus one local preference that remembers whether you chose light or dark mode.

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser. Related technologies — localStorage and sessionStorage — work similarly, storing data locally on your device rather than sending it back with every request. This page covers all of them.

2. What we actually set

NameTypePurposeDuration
themelocalStorage — preferenceRemembers your light or dark mode choiceUntil you clear it
__cf_bmCookie — strictly necessarySet by Cloudflare to distinguish humans from automated traffic and protect the siteAround 30 minutes
cf_clearanceCookie — strictly necessarySet by Cloudflare when a security challenge has been passed, so you are not challenged repeatedlySession to short-term

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No cross-site tracking pixels.
  • No selling or sharing of browsing behaviour with advertisers.
  • No social media tracking widgets embedded in the page.

4. Consent

Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent under the ePrivacy rules, because the Service cannot be delivered securely without them. The theme preference is stored on your own device at your request and is never transmitted to us.

If we later introduce analytics or marketing cookies, we will ask for your consent first, through a banner that lets you accept or refuse each category, and this page will be updated before that happens.

5. How to control cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking the Cloudflare security cookies may cause repeated security challenges or prevent the site from loading correctly. To clear the stored theme preference, clear site data for 3webobs.com in your browser.

6. Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as a technical consequence of serving the font files. No cookie is set by that request. If a payment step is used, the payment provider will set its own cookies on its own pages, governed by its own policy.

7. More information

How the resulting data is handled is described in our GDPR & Privacy Notice. Questions: contact@5thelement.ai.
