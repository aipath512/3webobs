For SEO & marketing agencies

Sell AI visibility. Without building the engine.

Your clients are already asking whether ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google’s AI Overviews mention them. Right now you’re answering with guesses. 3webs gives you an evidence-based answer instead — a report you can hand to a client without having to build the methodology yourself.

What you get

  • 167 signals, deterministic scoring. No score is written by a language model — every verdict traces to something actually fetched from the client’s site. That means you can defend every number in a client call.
  • A prioritised action plan. Failing signals are ranked by weight, so you can tell a client exactly what to fix first — and what to bill for next.
  • An HTTP API. POST /audit returns structured JSON. If you already have client dashboards, you can pull scores in directly instead of screenshotting a report.
  • Re-run on demand. Show before/after after you’ve done the work — the same URL in the same state always returns the same score, so improvements are provable, not just claimed.
