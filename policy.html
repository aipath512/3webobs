Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026 · Operator: AIVENTURE S.R.L., Romania

This Acceptable Use Policy sets out what you may and may not do with 3webobs.com and the Audit·AI™ service. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

The purpose is simple: the Service inspects third-party websites, so it must behave as a well-mannered visitor at all times. Anything that turns it into a nuisance, a weapon or a surveillance tool is prohibited.

1. How our crawler behaves

When you submit a URL, our infrastructure requests a limited set of publicly accessible resources from that domain. Specifically:

  • It requests the submitted page, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and a small, fixed list of declared signal files.
  • It identifies itself honestly in the user agent string.
  • It does not attempt to log in, submit forms, or access anything behind authentication.
  • It does not execute attacks, scans for vulnerabilities, or probe ports.
  • It applies request timeouts and does not retry aggressively.

2. Permitted use

  • Auditing a website you own or operate.
  • Auditing a website on behalf of a client who has authorised you.
  • Auditing a publicly accessible website for legitimate competitive, journalistic or research purposes, at reasonable volume.
  • Machine-to-machine access under an agreed arrangement, respecting agreed rate limits.

3. Prohibited use

You must not use the Service to:

  • Generate load intended to degrade, disrupt or deny service to any website.
  • Circumvent rate limits, quotas, access controls or paywalls — ours or anyone else's.
  • Scrape, harvest or reconstruct our signal methodology, catalogue or scoring logic for the purpose of building a competing product.
  • Resell, sublicense or white-label reports without a written licence.
  • Submit URLs at a volume or frequency that a reasonable operator would consider abusive.
  • Present a report as an official certification, legal compliance verdict, or endorsement by us of a third party.
  • Analyse a website in order to harass, defame, or build a case against a private individual.
  • Violate any applicable law, including data protection, computer misuse and unfair competition law.

4. Rate limits

We apply rate limits to protect both our infrastructure and the websites being analysed. Limits may be adjusted without notice. Sustained attempts to exceed them will result in throttling and may result in suspension.

5. If your website was audited and you object

If you operate a website and do not wish it to be analysed by our Service, contact us at contact@5thelement.ai with proof of control over the domain, and we will add it to an exclusion list. You may also block our user agent in your own robots.txt; we honour such directives.

6. Reporting abuse

If you believe the Service is being used against you or in breach of this Policy, write to contact@5thelement.ai with the relevant details. We investigate reports and act where a breach is established.

7. Consequences of breach

We may suspend or terminate access, without refund, where a serious or repeated breach of this Policy is established. Where conduct appears unlawful, we may report it to the competent authorities.
