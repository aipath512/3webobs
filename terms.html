2. What the Service does

2.1 Technical description

3webs analyses a website URL that you submit and returns a diagnostic report describing how that website presents itself to AI systems, answer engines and search crawlers.

2.1.1 What is fetched

The analysis is performed by fetching publicly accessible resources of the submitted website:

  • The HTML of the submitted page.
  • robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
  • A fixed, limited list of declared machine-readable signal files.
  • Structured data embedded in the page (JSON-LD, meta tags).

2.1.2 What is never attempted

The Service inspects only what is publicly served. It does not attempt authentication, does not bypass access controls, does not submit forms, and does not access private, internal or password-protected areas of any website.

2.2 Delivery

2.2.1 On-screen report

Results are displayed in the browser immediately after the scan completes.

2.2.2 Emailed report

Where you provide an email address, a copy of the report may be delivered to that address. Delivery depends on your mail provider and is not guaranteed.

3. Nature of the results

3.0 The report is a technical observation

It is not a certification, not a legal opinion and not a guarantee of outcome.

3.1 What a score represents

3.1.1 Signals actually tested

Scores reflect signals observable at the moment of the scan, evaluated against fixed rules.

3.1.2 Signals not applicable

Signals that cannot be verified from public sources are marked as not applicable rather than assigned an invented value. A report that shows many such markers is being honest about the limits of what was observable, not failing.

3.2 What we do not promise

We do not guarantee that acting on the report will produce any specific ranking, citation, visibility, traffic or commercial result. Third-party AI systems and search engines are outside our control and change their behaviour without notice.

3.3 Point-in-time validity

A report describes a website at a moment in time. It becomes progressively less accurate as the website, and the systems that consume it, change.
