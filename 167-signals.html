The 167 signals

The complete catalogue, exactly as the engine holds it · Last generated 31 August 2026

This is not a summary of the methodology. It is the registry itself: every signal the engine can evaluate, with the weight it carries in the score. Nothing is withheld, because a score you cannot audit is a score you should not trust.

Not every signal applies to every domain. Those that cannot be verified from public sources are returned as not applicable — never as failures — and each report states how many were actually tested. See the glossary for the vocabulary, and pricing for what a report costs.

How the weights work

Each signal carries a weight. The score for a dimension is the weighted average over the signals that could be tested in it, and the global score is the weighted average across dimensions. A signal returned as not applicable is removed from both the numerator and the denominator, so it neither helps nor hurts.

This is why the action plan orders failures by weight rather than by how easy they are to describe: fixing one heavy signal moves the number more than fixing five light ones.
