AI Signals

What are AI Signals?

AI Signals are machine-readable and access signals that help AI systems discover, retrieve, interpret, and reference a website.

Why does 3WEBOBS audit AI crawler access?

Crawler access determines whether relevant AI systems are technically permitted to retrieve public website content.

What does an llms.txt check tell me?

It records whether an llms.txt resource is present and usable as an explicit machine-facing navigation aid; it does not guarantee AI inclusion.

Why are machine-readable identity signals important?

They reduce ambiguity about the organization, website, services, and canonical resources exposed to automated systems.

Does passing AI Signals guarantee a ChatGPT citation?

No. Technical readiness can improve machine accessibility and interpretability, but no audit can guarantee selection or citation by an external AI system.

What does a missing AI Signal mean?

It means the audited signal was not observed in the captured evidence under the applicable test conditions.

Why does 3WEBOBS check AI-specific files separately from SEO?

AI-facing resources can serve machine discovery and interpretation purposes that are not equivalent to traditional search-engine optimization.

Can an AI Signal be present but incomplete?

Yes. A resource may exist while lacking expected fields, valid structure, useful content, or consistent references.

How should conflicting AI signals be handled?

Conflicting machine-readable statements should be reconciled so identity, permissions, URLs, and capabilities describe one coherent source of truth.

How often should AI Signals be re-audited?

They should be re-observed after material site changes and periodically because website configuration and external crawler behavior can change.

AEO

What does AEO mean in this audit?

Answer Engine Optimization measures signals that help answer-oriented systems extract concise, attributable answers from website content.

How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO primarily addresses search discovery and ranking signals, while AEO emphasizes answer extraction, clarity, entities, questions, and supporting evidence.

Why are direct answers useful for AEO?

A concise answer near a clearly stated question reduces the work required to identify a usable response passage.

What role do entities play in AEO?

Explicit entities help systems distinguish who or what a statement concerns and connect claims to the correct organization, service, person, or place.

Why does answer provenance matter?

Source, author, date, and supporting evidence help an answer system assess where a claim came from and whether it can be attributed.

Does FAQ content automatically improve AEO?

No. FAQs can improve question-answer clarity, but usefulness depends on relevance, accuracy, uniqueness, accessibility, and the rest of the page.

What causes an AEO signal to be unclear?

A signal can be unclear when evidence exists but is incomplete, ambiguous, inconsistent, or insufficient for a definite pass.

Should every page be written as questions and answers?

No. Q&A is useful where it matches user intent; forcing every page into FAQ form can reduce clarity and create repetitive content.

How does 3WEBOBS evaluate answer structure?

It observes predefined structural and content signals and records evidence for deterministic verdicts rather than asking a language model to invent a score.

Can AEO results change without changing the visible design?

Yes. Metadata, structured data, headings, machine-readable resources, and content semantics can change independently of visual design.

GEO

What does GEO mean on 3WEBOBS?

Generative Engine Optimization covers signals that can help generative systems understand, contextualize, and attribute a website's information.

How is GEO different from AEO?

AEO focuses on extractable answers; GEO more broadly addresses context, authority, evidence, entities, and material that can support generated responses.

Why are verifiable claims relevant to GEO?

Claims supported by identifiable evidence are easier to evaluate and attribute than unsupported promotional statements.

Does GEO mean writing content for robots instead of people?

No. Strong GEO keeps human-readable content primary while making its meaning, sources, entities, and relationships unambiguous to machines.

Why does source consistency matter for GEO?

Contradictory names, facts, URLs, or claims increase uncertainty when generative systems reconcile information about an entity.

Can off-site evidence affect GEO readiness?

Yes. Relevant external references can contribute context and authority, although 3WEBOBS only scores what its defined evidence and rules can observe.

What is a red GEO result?

It indicates that an applicable required signal was not observed according to the audit rule and captured evidence.

What should be fixed first after a weak GEO audit?

Prioritize missing high-impact evidence, identity consistency, source clarity, and technically inaccessible information before cosmetic optimization.

Does a high GEO score guarantee inclusion in AI answers?

No. External generative systems make their own retrieval and selection decisions; the score measures audited readiness signals, not guaranteed placement.

Why preserve historical GEO observations?

Historical observations make it possible to verify what signals were present at different times and correlate changes with later outcomes.

AIO

What does AIO measure?

AI Optimization measures website characteristics intended to improve machine comprehension, retrieval readiness, structured interpretation, and AI-facing usability.

How does AIO relate to AI Signals?

AI Signals are concrete machine-facing observations, while AIO evaluates a broader optimization layer that includes structure, context, accessibility, and interpretability.

Why is semantic HTML relevant to AIO?

Semantic structure exposes relationships such as headings, navigation, articles, and sections without requiring machines to infer them only from visual layout.

Why are canonical URLs important for AIO?

Canonical references reduce ambiguity about which URL represents the authoritative version of substantially similar content.

Can JavaScript-heavy pages create AIO problems?

They can when essential public information is unavailable or materially different without client-side execution accessible to the observing system.

What makes an AIO result yellow?

Yellow means the relevant signal is partial, incomplete, recommended but absent, or otherwise insufficient for an unambiguous green result under its rule.

Does AIO replace good website content?

No. Machine optimization cannot compensate for inaccurate, thin, obsolete, or unhelpful underlying information.

Why audit content accessibility and structure together?

A machine must first retrieve content and then correctly interpret its organization; failure at either layer reduces usable AI readiness.

Can AIO improvements be implemented without rebuilding a website?

Some can be added at the edge or metadata layer, while others require changes to the underlying content, templates, or application behavior.

How do I verify an AIO improvement?

Re-run the same defined observations, compare evidence and verdicts, and preserve before-and-after records rather than relying only on a visual inspection.

SEO

What does the SEO category measure?

It measures defined technical and on-page signals that affect conventional search-engine discoverability, crawlability, indexing context, and page interpretation.

Why is SEO still relevant in the AI era?

Search infrastructure remains a major discovery layer, and many machine systems also depend directly or indirectly on crawlable, well-structured web content.

Does 3WEBOBS replace a full SEO platform?

No. It audits its declared atomic signals; it is not a substitute for every keyword, backlink, rank-tracking, log-analysis, or competitive SEO tool.

Why are titles and descriptions audited?

They provide explicit page-level context and can reveal missing, duplicated, or poorly formed metadata relevant to discovery.

Why does sitemap quality matter?

A usable sitemap gives crawlers an explicit inventory of canonical URLs and can expose broken or inconsistent discovery paths.

What does robots.txt tell the SEO audit?

It exposes crawler directives that can permit or restrict access to website resources; it does not by itself prove that a page is indexed.

Why are internal links checked?

Internal links create navigable relationships between pages and help expose important content to both users and crawlers.

Does a green SEO signal mean the site ranks well?

No. Green means the audited condition passed; ranking depends on many additional content, authority, competition, query, and search-system factors.

What should a red SEO signal trigger?

Review the associated evidence and rule, determine whether the signal is applicable, then correct the underlying technical or content defect.

Why compare SEO observations over time?

A historical sequence can expose regressions after deployments and verify whether corrective work actually changed the audited signals.

A2A

What does A2A mean on 3WEBOBS?

Agent-to-Agent refers to machine-facing interfaces and declarations that allow software agents to discover capabilities and potentially interact with services.

How is A2A different from an AI-readable website?

An AI-readable site exposes information for interpretation; A2A additionally concerns explicit capabilities, interfaces, actions, policies, and machine-to-machine interaction.

What is an agent card?

An agent card is a machine-readable declaration describing an agent or service, including identity, capabilities, interfaces, or other discovery metadata.

Why audit capabilities separately from content?

Knowing what a business says is different from knowing what a machine endpoint declares it can do programmatically.

Does A2A readiness mean an agent may transact automatically?

No. Transactional behavior requires explicit interfaces, authorization, policy, security, and business controls beyond simple discoverability.

Why are policies important for A2A?

Policies define boundaries for machine interaction, including what is allowed, restricted, authenticated, or subject to human approval.

What does a missing A2A endpoint mean?

It means the expected machine interface was not observed at the tested location; it does not prove the organization has no private agent integration.

Why should A2A declarations be versioned?

Versioning lets agents and operators identify which contract or capability definition they are relying on as interfaces evolve.

How does verification support A2A?

Cryptographic provenance can let another machine verify that a retrieved observation or declaration has not changed since its recorded artifact was produced.

Is A2A necessary for every business today?

No. Its relevance depends on whether the business intends to expose services or capabilities to software agents; the audit should distinguish applicability from absence.

Mixed Signals — Schema.org

Why is Schema.org called Mixed Signals?

Schema.org is read across conventional search and AI-oriented systems, so its structured data cuts across multiple discovery categories rather than belonging to only one.

What does the Schema.org checklist contain?

It contains 100 predefined structured-data elements that 3WEBOBS checks for presence, completeness, applicability, and importance.

What does a green Schema.org hook mean?

Green means the element was observed and met the completeness conditions defined for that checklist item.

What does a yellow Schema.org result mean?

Yellow indicates an incomplete observed element or an absent recommended element that does not meet the conditions for a red failure.

What does a red Schema.org result mean?

Red indicates an applicable required element is absent, or an applicable conditional requirement is missing.

What does grey mean in the Schema.org table?

Grey identifies an optional absent element or an item that is not applicable, so it is excluded from the graded denominator.

How is the Schema.org score calculated?

Graded green items receive full credit, yellow items half credit, and red items no credit; grey items are excluded from the graded total.

Does more Schema.org markup always produce a better result?

No. Structured data should accurately describe real page content and applicable entities; irrelevant or misleading markup is not an improvement.

Why can Schema.org be present but still yellow?

A type or property may exist while required or expected supporting fields are incomplete, inconsistent, or missing.

Does valid Schema.org guarantee rich results or AI citations?

No. Valid structured data improves explicit machine-readable meaning, but external platforms independently decide whether and how to use it.
